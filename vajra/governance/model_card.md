# VAJRA GATE — model card

**What it is.** A two-persona payment-fraud detector (GATE-I payer/issuer side, GATE-B beneficiary
side) trained on simulated data. One codebase, two deployment personae.

**Intended use.** DEFENSIVE research and stress-testing. The archive's unit of content is a
kill-chain shape and observable signatures in payment data, not a procedure. Recipient policy is
scoped to financial institutions, networks, regulators and CERT-In-style bodies.

**Training data.** Wholly synthetic (`vajra-sim`). Every identifier is synthetic and non-routable; no
real cardholder PII, ever. Real public data (IEEE-CIS projection) anchors the LEGITIMATE population on
exactly two conditionals (T1); nothing on the India rails our thesis rests on is T1 — those are T3,
mechanistically enforced.

**Evaluation.** Time-forward splits with purge/embargo, entity-disjoint folds, sealed-family holdout,
sibling-transfer recall. Metrics: recall @ fixed FPR, PR-AUC, precision@k at staffed capacity,
value-detection rate, per-family recall, recall by entity age, HARD-BENIGN false-positive rates. See
`reports/metrics.md`.

**Limitations.** Base rates are swept policy parameters, not observations. Recall figures do NOT
transfer to a production issuer portfolio; view-restricted recall is published via the visibility
ablation, not extrapolated. Latency is measured on laptop-class hardware and not extrapolated to
network scale [VERIFY on real hardware]. Circularity is bounded, not eliminated.

**Fairness.** No protected attributes or obvious proxies (`tests/test_no_protected_attributes.py`
asserts it — see `features/registry.yaml`). A disparate-impact table over decline, friction AND
beneficiary-side hold/freeze rates by segment/geography/device tier is generated by `make eval`; its
segments are synthetic attributes we generated, so it demonstrates the reporting machinery exists, not
that a real portfolio is fair.

**Governance.** Append-only hash-chained decision log (`gate/decision.py::hash_chain_entry`), fixed
~40-string reason-code vocabulary, feature registry with lineage, dual-use lint reject log
(`governance/dual_use_reject_log.json`). Rollout ladder: shadow → challenger on a slice → friction-only
→ guardrailed decline with automatic rollback → kill switch to G0-only.
